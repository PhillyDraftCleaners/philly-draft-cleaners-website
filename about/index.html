Our story

About Philly Draft Cleaners

We're Micro Matic Dispense Institute certified technicians dedicated to one thing: making sure every pour in Philadelphia tastes exactly as the brewer intended.

Why we do this

We Love the Philly Beer Scene

Local brewers put months of hard work into perfecting a keg — but all that effort can be ruined in the last few feet between the keg and the glass. Consumers deserve to taste the beer exactly as the brewer intended. That's what keeps us going.

We don't cut corners. Our technicians are certified by the Micro Matic Dispense Institute, meaning we understand the exact science behind keg-to-glass technology. We strictly follow Brewers Association Draught Quality standards to give your bar a premium, deep clean — not just a quick flush.

For us, line cleaning isn't just maintenance. It's how we protect the brewer's art and your bar's reputation.

What sets us apart

The Philly Draft Cleaners Difference

01

Certified Expertise

Micro Matic Dispense Institute certification means we understand the full keg-to-glass system — temperature, pressure, carbonation, line materials, and cleaning chemistry — and we apply that knowledge on every visit.

02

The Right Equipment

We use premium electric recirculating pumps — not pressure pots. The recirculating method creates turbulent flow that physically scrubs the interior walls of your lines. That's 80 times more effective than a static soak, and there's no shortcut that replicates it.

03

By the Book, Every Time

We follow the Brewers Association Draught Quality Manual without exception. You get a written cleaning log on every visit — not just when you ask for one. Your records are always current and ready for inspection.
